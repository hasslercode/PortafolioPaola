## Who this is for

Entrepreneurs and brands in Colombia that already shoot (or can shoot) but need publish-ready short-form videos with a consistent premium look.

## What's included

- Rush organization and shot selection
- Hook → story → CTA structure
- Retention-oriented creative editing
- Burned-in captions and brand typography
- Light motion graphics when useful
- Royalty-free music and color consistency
- Publish-ready exports (9:16)

## What's not included

- Full content strategy research ([content strategy](/en/services/content-strategy))
- Daily community management ([monthly management](/en/services/monthly-management))
- Paid media buying
- Multi-camera on-site production (unless scoped separately)
