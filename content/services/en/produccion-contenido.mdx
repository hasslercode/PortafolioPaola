If you are looking for **social video editing**, **Reels production**, **TikTok editing** or **brand short-form video** in Colombia, this service is the bridge between "I have footage" and "I have premium pieces that retain and drive action."

I do not sell generic CapCut hours or recycled templates. I design editing with commercial intent: a hook in the first seconds, short storytelling, mobile-readable captions, a brand visual system and a clear CTA at the end. Every cut answers a concrete question: why should someone in Bogotá, Medellín or Cali keep watching instead of scrolling past?

Demand for **social video editing in Colombia** has grown because algorithms reward short-form video and punish visual inconsistency. Founders, SMBs and mid-size brands already know how to shoot — often on a phone — but lose reach when post-production looks amateur, when there are no captions (most people watch without sound) or when every piece looks like it was made by a different person. ## What problem it solves

Searches for "content creation," "video editing" and "social media videos" are everywhere in Colombia. The real pain is usually the same regardless of sector:

1. **You shoot a lot and publish little.** Rushes pile up on your phone or Drive because nobody has time to cut with criteria.

**Strategic editing** addresses all four. It is not decoration: it is the layer where many Colombian brands win or lose organic reach. A well-edited Reels can multiply the reach of mediocre footage; the reverse is also true — excellent raw material badly cut dies in the feed.

## Retention: what separates premium editing from basic trimming

When I talk about **retention**, I mean the percentage of people still watching after second 3, 5 and 10. Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts use that signal to decide whether to show your piece to more people. That is why social video editing in Colombia cannot be "add music and export."

Editing decisions that impact retention include:

- **Visual and verbal hook in 1–3 seconds.** Promise, tension, result or question — before the user swipes away.
- **Cut pacing.** Remove dead pauses, long breaths and repetition. Every shot should advance the story or reinforce the message.
- **Burned-in captions.** In Colombia, as elsewhere, most people consume video without sound on transit, at work or in bed. Without readable captions, you lose message and retention.
- **Visual hierarchy.** On-screen text that reinforces audio, not word-for-word duplication without criteria.
- **Consistent opening pattern.** The viewer recognizes your brand in the feed before reading the username.
- **CTA at the close.** Follow, save, DM, book, buy — explicit and aligned with batch objective.

Each delivery includes brief notes on what worked in the pilot piece and what to iterate in the next batch. Retention improves with systems, not luck.

## UGC vs editing: two pieces of the same puzzle

A frequent confusion in Colombia is mixing **UGC (User Generated Content)** with **professional editing**. They are complementary, not interchangeable.

**UGC** is content created by people — customers, micro-influencers or creators — who talk about or demonstrate your product with authentic tone, like a real recommendation. The value is on-camera credibility and social proof. Hiring a UGC creator answers: "who records something believable about my brand?"

**Social video editing** answers a different question: "how do I turn those recordings — or mine — into pieces that retain, look premium and drive action?" Even the most authentic UGC loses impact if audio is low, the hook takes eight seconds, there are no captions and the close has no CTA.

In practice, many Colombian brands combine both:

1. A UGC creator records testimonials, unboxings or "day using the product" clips.
2. You or your team record brand pieces, behind-the-scenes or objection-handling content.
3. This service unifies everything under a **visual system** — typography, color, pacing, music — so the feed feels coherent even with multiple voices behind it.

If you only hire UGC without strategic post-production, you get raw authenticity. If you only edit without brief or angle, you get pretty videos that do not convert. The ideal flow: strategy (optional but recommended) → recording (yours, your team's or UGC) → premium editing → publishing (yours or via monthly management).

## How we work: step-by-step process

### 1. Brief and initial alignment

Before touching a timeline, we define batch objective: awareness, social proof, education, offer, launch. Without that, editing becomes personal taste. Use the brief template below or complete the form on [contact](/en/contact).

### 2. Material intake

I receive rushes via Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer — whatever you use. Also brand references: logo, palette, Reels examples you like (and ones you do not), tone of voice. If you have a [content strategy](/en/services/content-strategy) document, even better: it speeds decisions.

### 3. Hero cut (pilot piece)

I edit **one representative piece** from the pack. Here we align pacing, typography, captions, music and motion level. This is the most important checkpoint: if the hero works, the rest of the batch flows with the same system.

### 4. Pack production

With the approved standard, I edit the remaining pieces maintaining visual coherence. Each video has its hook and CTA, but the "system" feels unified in the feed.

### 5. Delivery and revisions

I deliver exports ready for Instagram, TikTok or Shorts. I apply the revision rounds agreed in the proposal. Scope changes — extra pieces, reshoots — are quoted separately.

### 6. Iteration (optional)

If we work recurrently, each batch informs the next: which hooks retained more, which CTAs generated DMs, which formats to repeat. Editing improves with data, not just intuition.
