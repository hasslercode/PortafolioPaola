## What it is
An editing-only service. You record; I edit so every piece has a spectacular finish.

## Actions
- Rush organization and take selection
- Creative editing paced for retention
- Burned-in captions and brand typography
- Lower-thirds, typographic stickers and light motion graphics
- Royalty-free music and color grading
- Publish-ready export (vertical 9:16 when applicable)
- One revision round per piece

## Consideration
Investment is based on the duration of content to edit. No fixed price — the proposal is tailored to each company.

## What’s not included
- Content research or strategy
- Scriptwriting from scratch
- Community management / replies
- Paid media or ad management

## Expected outcome
Publish-ready pieces with a premium standard, built from footage you already have.
