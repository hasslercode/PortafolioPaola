## What it is
The client already knows what to say. I simply make it look spectacular.

## Includes
- Rush organization and take selection
- Creative editing paced for retention
- Burned-in captions and brand typography
- Light motion graphics and lower-thirds
- Royalty-free music and color grading
- Publish-ready export (9:16 when applicable)

## Consideration
You provide the recorded footage. The proposal responds to duration and volume.

## What’s not included
- Content research or strategy
- Scriptwriting from scratch
- Community management
- Paid media

## Expected outcome
Publish-ready pieces with a premium finish, built from footage you already have.
