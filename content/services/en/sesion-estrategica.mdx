## A direct answer if you're looking for strategic consulting in Colombia

If you searched for **social media consulting**, **digital advisory** or a **strategy session** to decide how to invest in content in Colombia, this is the most honest entry point I offer.

It is not a post package. It is not community management dressed up as strategy. It is one focused meeting with me — with prep before and a deliverable after — where we review what you have today, identify real opportunities and build an action plan you can execute calmly.

I've supported consumer and retail brands in contexts where organic content actually moves the needle: Coca-Cola, Starbucks, H&M, TOTTO, Cine Colombia and experiences within the Parque Alegra ecosystem in Barranquilla, with results such as **+1.3M organic views** on retail campaigns. That background doesn't mean your brand needs the same volume; it means I know how to read what works in short-form content and what is noise.

## What the strategy session is

The strategy session is **concentrated digital consulting**. I work as a content strategist, not as your account operator.

The flow:

1. You share business context and commercial goals.
2. We review your social channels together (or the absence of them).
3. We spot gaps, opportunities and risks.
4. We prioritize actions by impact vs. effort.
5. I deliver an action plan you can use immediately.

I don't sell hype or viral promises. I sell **clarity** — the same clarity you need before hiring production, a community manager or an agency offering "12 posts per month" without asking why.

## Who it's for

This service fits especially well if you recognize yourself here:

- **Entrepreneurs and SMEs in Colombia** who post often but don't see business results.
- **Personal brands** (consultants, coaches, creatives) who want to fix their message before scaling production.
- **Small marketing teams** that need expert second opinion before presenting an internal plan.
- **Local businesses** (retail, food, services) comparing a cheap CM vs. investing with judgment.
- **Brands that already have a community manager** but suspect strategic direction is missing upstream.
- **Founders about to launch** who prefer a compass before spending on cameras, editors or ads.

If you already know the direction and only need hands to execute, [content strategy](/en/services/content-strategy) or [monthly management](/en/services/monthly-management) may fit better. The session is for when the main question is: *what do we do, and in what order?*

## What problem it solves

In Colombia, searches for "community manager", "content creation" and "digital consulting" grow every year. The pattern I see repeatedly:

1. **Posting without objective.** Pretty, consistent, irrelevant to sales.
2. **Copying viral formats** without adapting them to brand or buyer.
3. **Fragmented vendors:** one designs, another edits, nobody connects to business.
4. **Hiring cheap** and receiving a generic calendar that could belong to any brand.
5. **Analysis paralysis:** months thinking, zero pieces published.

The strategy session hits step zero: **decide with data and judgment** before executing.

## Step-by-step process

### 1. Preparation (before the meeting)

I send a short questionnaire: sector, average ticket, commercial goal for the quarter, active platforms, available team and rough budget. I also ask for read access to profiles or screenshots if you don't have a presence yet.

That lets me arrive with preliminary observations — not to judge, but to use our time well.

### 2. Live session (90–120 minutes)

By video call we review:

- **Channel diagnosis:** visual consistency, frequency, hooks, CTAs, bio, highlights.
- **Audience and competition:** who follows you vs. who should, what your category does in Colombia.
- **Existing content:** what to keep, archive or repeat with improvements.
- **Simple funnel:** awareness → consideration → conversion for your business model.
- **Quick wins:** low-effort actions with visible return in 2–4 weeks.

The conversation is direct. I'd rather tell you your feed is messy than hand you a plan you won't execute.

### 3. Action plan delivery

Within a few days you receive a summary document with:

- Main findings from the diagnosis.
- Prioritized opportunities (max 5–7 focus areas).
- Format and platform recommendations.
- Concrete next steps: what you do, what to outsource, in what order.
- Suggested next service if relevant (no hard sell).

When we're done you should be able to answer, without doubt:

- Why am I on social, and what am I NOT chasing this quarter?
