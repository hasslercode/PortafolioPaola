## What monthly management is

**Monthly management** is a recurring plan integrating layers you'd normally hire separately:

- Monthly direction (light strategy, not a full annual document).
- Production or piece coordination (video, carousels, stories per agreement).
- Premium editing when the format requires it.
- Scheduling and publishing on agreed platforms.
- Follow-up and metric review with recommendations for the next month.

It's the right format when you need **consistent presence** but don't want to edit at midnight, or depend on a junior CM who doesn't know your margin or buyer.

If you only need point clarity, start with [strategy session](/en/services/strategy-session). If you need a deep strategic document once, [content strategy](/en/services/content-strategy). If your team publishes and you only need editing, [content production](/en/services/content-production). Monthly management is when you want to **delegate the full cycle**.

## Month-by-month process

### Week 0 — Onboarding (first month or rebrief only)

Alignment on commercial goals, access, brand guides, approval flow, tone, legal restrictions (promotions, claims) and KPI definition. If a [content strategy](/en/services/content-strategy) document exists, we adopt it as base.

### Week 1 — Light monthly strategy

I define month priorities: campaigns, launches, commercial dates (Mother's Day, local seasons), key messages and format mix. Not a 40-page PDF — executable direction for the next four weeks.

### Weeks 1–3 — Production and editing

Filming coordination (with you, your team or creator brief), material selection, editing with hook and mobile-first subtitles, platform adaptations if needed.

### Weeks 3–4 — Scheduling, publishing and follow-up

I schedule per agreed windows, publish on authorized profiles, monitor basic organic performance and prepare learnings for next month.

### Month close — Review and recommendations

Honest summary: what worked, what didn't, what to try. No invented metrics — real platform data.

## What's included

- Light monthly strategy (priorities, messages, operational calendar).
- Production/coordination of contracted pieces.
- Premium short-video editing when the plan includes it.
- Conversion-oriented copy within your brand tone.
- Scheduling and publishing on defined platforms (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn per agreement).

- Monthly close meeting or report with recommendations.

## What's not included

- Full annual strategic document (pillars from scratch, deep research) → contract separately as [content strategy](/en/services/content-strategy).
- Editing-only batches without publishing → [content production](/en/services/content-production).
- Media buying / Meta, TikTok, Google ads.
- In-store customer service, POS, logistics or phone sales.
- Intensive community care (crisis, high DM volume, technical support) without add-on.
- Commercial music rights or complex talent/image contracts without separate budget.
- Virality guarantee or fixed follower growth.

Clear scope avoids the classic "I thought that was included."
