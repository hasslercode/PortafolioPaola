## Content strategy: the compass before you produce in Colombia

If you're looking for **content strategy** for Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn or a channel mix in Colombia, this is where I work exclusively as a **content strategist** — without mixing editing, daily publishing or generic community care.

The premise is simple: **producing without strategy is expensive**. Every misaligned Reel, every pretty carousel that doesn't sell and every month of commodity CM adds cost without learning. A well-built strategic document orders message, format, cadence and metrics before you spend on cameras, editors or ads.

I've built content for consumer and retail brands in Colombia — Coca-Cola, Starbucks, H&M, TOTTO, Cine Colombia and experiences within Parque Alegra (Barranquilla) — with measurable organic results, including campaigns with **+1.3M organic views** in a retail context. That experience informs how I think about short-form hooks and commercial storytelling; it doesn't mean your deliverable includes production for those brands, but that strategy comes from real practice in competitive categories.

## What content strategy is

**Content strategy** is documented consulting. It is not a generic post calendar or a 100-slide PDF nobody opens.

It answers:

- Who is your audience and what matters when they discover you on social?
- What does your brand promise in content — and what should it not promise?
- Which pillars sustain your presence for months, not just one week?
- Which formats and platforms do you prioritize given resources and commercial goals?
- How do you measure whether content moves sales, bookings, leads or recognition?

I work in first person with you (and your team if applicable) to translate business goals into an editorial plan you can execute — yourself, with a CM, with an editor or with me on [content production](/en/services/content-production) or [monthly management](/en/services/monthly-management).

Searches for "content strategy", "content plan" and "content strategist Colombia" often hide concrete frustrations:

1. **Brand inconsistency:** every piece looks like a different company.

## Work process

### Phase 1 — Immersion and brief

I collect brand information, offer, rough margins (if shared), direct and indirect competitors, content history and basic metrics access. If you started with a [strategy session](/en/services/strategy-session), I reuse findings to avoid duplicate work.

### Phase 2 — Research and analysis

- **Digital brand audit:** visual consistency, voice, perceived value proposition.
- **Competitive analysis:** what your category does in Colombia and where differentiation space exists.
- **Audience mapping:** segments, pains, objections, content consumption moments.
- **Format benchmark:** structures that retain in your niche (hook, length, CTA).

### Phase 3 — Strategic architecture

I define editorial pillars (usually 3–5), recommended cadence, format mix (Reels, carousels, stories, TikTok, LinkedIn articles, etc.), content funnel and KPIs by stage.

### Phase 4 — Calendar and ideas

I build an initial editorial calendar (typically 4–8 weeks depending on scope) and a prioritized idea bank with angle, suggested format and objective. Not 200 loose titles — executable, prioritized ideas.

### Phase 5 — Presentation and revisions

Delivery session to walk through the document, team Q&A and one included revision round. You leave knowing how to use the deliverable the following Monday.

## What's included

- Brand and digital positioning analysis.
- Competitor map relevant to content (not just pricing).
- **Operational buyer persona:** motivations, objections, language, channels.
- **Content pillars** with description, examples and suggested weight.
- **Initial editorial calendar** with dates, formats and themes.
- **Prioritized idea bank** with angle and orientational CTA.
- **Format and platform recommendations** (effort vs. impact).
- **Goals and metrics** by funnel (awareness, consideration, conversion).
- **Tone guidelines** and copy/visual direction.
- Production notes: what to shoot, what to avoid, style references.
