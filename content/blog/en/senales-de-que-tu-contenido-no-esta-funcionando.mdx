## Short answer

Signs your content is not working: Seguir igual and Auditar y ajustar are not the same role though many proposals blur them. This comparison helps you decide by stage, budget and commercial goal.

## Clear definitions

**Seguir igual:** typical deliverables and limits.

**Auditar y ajustar:** when it adds strategic vs tactical value.

## Quick comparison

| Criteria | Seguir igual | Auditar y ajustar |
|----------|---------|--------|
| Main focus | Execution | Strategy/system |
| Best when | You need volume | You need positioning |

## How to decide

Do you have clear pillars and buyer persona?

Is your bottleneck ideas, production or conversion?

How many monthly pieces must you sustain?

## Extended decision matrix

Score 1–5: internal strategic clarity, monthly volume, budget, urgency, brand maturity. Low internal strategy + high urgency often needs Auditar y ajustar first.

## Interview questions

Which KPIs do they report? Who writes scripts? File ownership? Revision rounds? Vertical experience? Ask for metrics case, not only pretty screenshots.

## Contracts and scope

Define monthly deliverables, channels, response times, cadence failure terms and exit period.

## Commercial bridge

See [pricing](/en/pricing) and [content strategy](/en/services/content-strategy). Many brands hire Seguir igual when they need Auditar y ajustar.

## Success signals at 90 days

Stable or growing qualified inquiries, reusable content library, clear reporting.

## Recommended hybrid

Quarterly strategist + monthly executor + batch production is common for scaling SMBs.

## Operational mistakes

No UGC image rights contract. No brand-owned account access. No asset backup.

## Final checklist

Role defined, KPI agreed, budget aligned to ticket, 60-day review scheduled.

## Total cost of ownership

Include internal brief time, revisions, tools and lost opportunity if the wrong role delays sales.

## Migrating between models

Start with Seguir igual for 90 days and move to Auditar y ajustar when volume grows — document handoff.

## Red flags in proposals

Very low price without detail, guaranteed virality, no verifiable references, refusal to report business KPIs.

## Tracking board

Columns: month, role hired, pieces delivered, inquiries, cost, notes. Review before renewing.

## Brand impact

Wrong provider can dilute positioning — prioritize fit with [content strategy](/en/services/content-strategy) over visual trend.

## Closing questions

What happens month 1 if no results? Who approves scripts? Is there comment/DM response SLA?

## Weekly application of Signs your content is not working

Each Monday review last week's posts and classify by outcome: high reach, high saves, DMs, sales. Duplicate the winning format with a new angle — do not copy the script verbatim.

## Formats that work in Colombia

9:16 Reels with burned captions remain discovery base. Carousels handle objections. Stories maintain daily relationship. Pick based on real capacity — see [content production](/en/services/content-production) if editing is the bottleneck.

## Advanced mistakes

Mixing languages without brand criteria confuses. Posting offers without prior proof burns audience. Ignoring intent comments in the first 2 hours hurts distribution.

## Internal collaboration

Define roles: who ideates, shoots, edits, publishes, replies. A 45-minute weekly meeting prevents bottlenecks.

## Quarterly review

Every 90 days ask: are pillars and persona still valid? Which format drove most business? Adjust organic vs paid budget using [organic content ROI](/en/blog/roi-contenido-organico).

## Typical Colombian SMB

A local business with medium ticket often needs 8–12 monthly pieces and fast WhatsApp response. After 10 weeks of cadence many double inquiries though followers grow slowly.
