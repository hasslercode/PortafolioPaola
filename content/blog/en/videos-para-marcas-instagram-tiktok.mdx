## Short answer

**Brand videos for Instagram and TikTok** in Colombia are the main organic discovery engine in 2026. You do not compete with Netflix: you compete with infinite scroll entertainment. You win with immediate hook, concrete proof, mandatory captions and CTA connecting to WhatsApp, store or landing.

This guide combines strategy, production and post-production for B2C brands, retail and founders who already know static feed is not enough.

## Why short video dominates in Colombia

Colombian consumers spend hours on Reels and TikTok. Consumer brands — Coca-Cola, Starbucks, H&M, TOTTO, Cine Colombia — use short video for brand moments, promos and retail storytelling. Highlighted campaigns in ecosystems like Parque Alegra have exceeded **+1.3M organic views** in 90 days when system (format + cadence + proof) aligns.

For SMEs, the lesson is not copying corporate budget: it is copying **format discipline** — hook, value, CTA — with own resources or [outsourced production](/en/services/content-production).

## Instagram Reels vs TikTok: practical differences

| Factor | Instagram Reels | TikTok |
|---|---|---|
| Audience | Broad mix, purchase via DM/web | Strong discovery, younger audience |
| Aesthetic | Polished + aspirational tolerated | Native, raw, authentic rewarded |
| Sweet spot duration | 15–60 s | 15–45 s (short loops) |
| Hashtags | Moderate, caption SEO | Less critical, on-screen SEO |
| Typical goal | Community + conversion | Awareness + A/B proof |

**Smart production:** shoot once, export 9:16 master, adapt on-screen hook text and caption per platform. Do not reuse without adjusting first frame — each app has different UI.

## Anatomy of a brand video that retains

### Seconds 0–3: Hook

Proven options:
- Uncomfortable question (“Why does your ___ fail?”)
- Specific promise (“In 30 seconds I’ll show…”)
- Visual pattern interrupt (cut, zoom, unexpected object)
- Quick social proof (“How we helped ___ in Medellín”)

Without hook, the rest does not exist.

### Seconds 3–20: Value or tension

Demo, mini storytelling, quick list, before/after. One strong idea per video — not three messages.

### Seconds 20–45: Proof and CTA

Testimonial, verifiable data, result, clear offer. Close with action: comment, save, write, link.

### Entire video: Captions

Much consumption is silent on commute, office or home. Large high-contrast captions without blocking bottom safe area.

Go deeper on editing in [social video editing Colombia](/en/blog/edicion-de-videos-para-redes-colombia).

## Video formats by brand objective

### Awareness — trend with angle

Use trending audio only if it connects to promise. Adapt to Colombian local context.

### Consideration — education / myths

“3 myths about…”, “How to choose…”, “Mistakes when buying…”

### Conversion — demo + offer

Product in use, honest comparison, real urgency (stock, season).

### Retention — weekly series

“Chapter 2”, “Day 5 using…”, continuity inviting profile follow.

### Social proof — UGC and testimonials

## Pre-production: brand video brief

Before shooting, document:
- Objective (awareness / consideration / conversion)
- Primary platform
- Written hook
- Bullet script or 3-frame storyboard
- Proof included
- CTA and destination (WA, link, comment)
- References (2 competitor or inspiration videos)

If brief is missing, return to [content strategy](/en/blog/estrategia-de-contenido-colombia) or [social content creation](/en/blog/creacion-de-contenido-para-redes-sociales).

## Recording: specs and practice

**Minimum kit:** recent smartphone + tripod + lapel mic

**Intermediate kit:** mirrorless camera + LED light + audio monitor

**Colombia tips:**
- Avoid shooting by traffic windows without directional mic
- In retail, get permission and avoid ambient copyrighted music in edit
- Outdoors: golden hour; avoid harsh midday on coast/Caribbean

**Useful shots:** wide context, medium presenter, close product, B-roll hands/process, text insert on props.

## Post-production that moves metrics

1. **First cut before second 1** — eliminate dead air
2. **Jump cuts** to speech rhythm
3. **Subtle zooms** on emphasis
4. **Lower third** with brand (do not abuse)
5. **Music low** under voice (-18 to -22 dB under voice)
6. **Consistent color** with feed
7. **Export** 9:16, 30 fps, adequate bitrate (avoid horrible compression)

Editing is not cosmetic: it is retention. Outsource packs if volume exceeds internal capacity.

**Fashion / retail:** try-on, transitions, outfit in urban context (Medellín, Bogotá)

**Food:** ASMR, first bite, kitchen BTS, Monday promo

**Beauty:** texture, routine, honest before/after

**Services:** founder on camera, client case, FAQ video

## Mistakes that kill brand videos

1. 5-second animated logo at start
2. “Hi I’m…” intro without hook
3. Small illegible text
4. Music louder than voice
5. Absent or generic “follow us” CTA
6. Horizontal video badly cropped to 9:16
7. Caption copy does not reinforce hook
8. Same file on IG and TT without A/B test

## Monthly production calendar (SME)

**Week 1:** planning + briefs (4 videos)
**Week 2:** batch recording
**Week 3:** editing + brand review
**Week 4:** publishing + analysis + 2 react pieces (trend/objection)

Cadence: 2–4 videos/week by capacity. Consistency > sporadic spikes.

## Metrics per platform

**Instagram Reels:** plays, watch time, saves, shares, profile visits

**TikTok:** average watch time, rewatches, qualitative comments, follower conversion

Cross with [organic content ROI](/en/blog/roi-contenido-organico). Compare pieces against each other, not invented benchmarks.

## Paid + organic: same video, two roles

- **Organic:** social proof, community, social SEO
- **Paid:** retargeting, lookalike, offer — use UGC for CTR

Whitelisting UGC when contract allows. See [UGC Colombia guide](/en/blog/creador-ugc-colombia-guia).

## 30-second script template

```
[0-3s] HOOK: "Still buying ___ without knowing if it works?"
[3-15s] VALUE: Show problem + product demo
[15-25s] PROOF: "In 2 weeks, clients in Bogotá report ___"
[25-30s] CTA: "Comment QUIERO and I'll send guide on WhatsApp"
```

Adjust timing by real audience retention.
