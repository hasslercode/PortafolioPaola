## Definition

**Buyer persona** is a semi-fictional profile of the ideal buyer based on real data.

For brands and SMBs in Colombia, Buyer persona connects to strategy, production and measurement.

## Why it matters

Understanding Buyer persona avoids vendor misunderstandings and vanity metrics without sales impact.

## Practical example

An SMB tags each weekly piece with Buyer persona and reviews in a 30-minute Friday meeting.
