## Definition

The **editorial calendar** plans what to publish, when, in which format and with which CTA.

For brands and SMBs in Colombia, Editorial calendar connects to strategy, production and measurement.

## Why it matters

Understanding Editorial calendar avoids vendor misunderstandings and vanity metrics without sales impact.

## Practical example

An SMB tags each weekly piece with Editorial calendar and reviews in a 30-minute Friday meeting.
