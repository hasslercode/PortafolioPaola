## Short answer

**Reels editing price in Colombia** in 2026 has no official single rate — it depends on editorial complexity, volume, deadlines and whether brand visual system is included. This guide orients realistic ranges for SMEs, what to request in quotes and when to invest premium vs basic.

Ranges are compass, not absolute truth. Quote documented scope.

## Orientative ranges by edit type

| Type | Includes | COP/piece range |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | Cut, music, corrected auto subs | $80,000 – $150,000 |
| Standard | Retention rhythm, styled subs, visual CTA | $150,000 – $250,000 |
| Premium | Motion, lower thirds, color grade, B-roll | $250,000 – $450,000 |
| Hero / campaign | Storyboard, sfx, multiple layers | $450,000 – $800,000+ |

Twenty-four to forty-eight-hour urgency usually +20–50%. Tier-1 cities (Bogotá, Medellín) tend upper range.

## Monthly packs: volume discount

| Pack | Pieces/month | Orientative total COP |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 4–6 | $500,000 – $900,000 |
| Growth | 8–12 | $1,200,000 – $2,400,000 |
| Scale | 16–20 | $2,400,000 – $4,000,000+ |

Growth pack ~$150,000–$200,000 per standard piece vs $200,000+ single order.

See [monthly content pack how many videos](/en/blog/pack-de-contenido-mensual-cuantos-videos).

## What pushes price up

Custom recurring motion graphics; multiple export formats (9:16 + 1:1 + 16:9); unlimited revisions (avoid); premium licensed music included; bilingual captions; twenty-four-hour delivery; source project + SRT + covers; editing disorganized raw footage without brief.

Clear brief lowers effective price. See [how to brief editor](/en/blog/como-briefear-a-tu-editor-de-video).

## What professional quote should include

1. Piece count and target duration each
2. Brand-style burned captions
3. Revisions (typically two rounds)
4. Music (included library or client provides)
5. [Instagram/TikTok export specs](/en/blog/exportar-video-para-instagram-y-tiktok)
6. Weekly or bi-weekly delivery
7. Paid usage rights
8. Exclusions (filming, script, talent)

Unclear exclusions create surprise extras.

## Suspicious price red flags

$20,000/piece "professional" — unsustainable quality; no retention portfolio; unlimited revisions without contract; no brief or objective questions; vertical-only badly exported; copyright music without disclaimer.

Cheap that does not retain costs more than premium that generates messages.

## How to evaluate editing ROI

| Metric | Before internal edit | After editor |
|---|---|---|
| 3s retention | 45% | 62% |
| Saves/video | 12 | 34 |
| WA chats/week | 3 | 11 |

If $200,000/piece editor generates eight extra chats selling $80,000 product, ROI turns positive fast.

## Healthy negotiation

Ask three-month pack ten to fifteen percent discount; pay on time — freelancers prioritize; consolidate feedback one round; monthly brief bank reduces setup; do not negotiate revisions to zero — quality drops.

## Minimum contract

Scope pieces/month, price and payment, delivery deadlines, revision count, output IP, confidentiality, cancellation. Protects both sides.

## Hidden cost of internal editing

CM $1,500,000/month spending forty percent on edit = $600,000 equivalent plus lost sales/community opportunity. Outsourcing $1,200,000 pack of ten pieces may free CM.

Calculate real hours, not just freelancer rate.

## When to pay premium

Product hero launch; high retail season campaign; ads with creative fatigue needing differentiated motion; premium fashion/beauty brand; flagship case study video. Day-to-day: standard sufficient with brand system.

## Quoting mistakes

Compare price without deliverables; no rhythm references; ask fifteen pieces at price of eight; change script post v1 without paying extra; undefined licensed music responsibility.
