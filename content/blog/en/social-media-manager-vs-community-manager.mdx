## Short answer

In Colombia, searches for what a community manager is — and SMM vs CM — are rising. Confused titles lead to bad hires.

| Role | Focus | Typical delivery |
|------|-------|------------------|
| **Community manager** | Ops + community | Publishing, inbox, moderation |
| **Social media manager** | Channel coordination | Calendar, briefs, reporting |
| **Content strategist** | System | Pillars, messages, KPIs |
| **Creator / editor** | Assets | Reels, UGC, design |

If you need short-form growth, a CM alone is not enough — see [CM vs strategist](/en/blog/community-manager-vs-estratega-digital) and [who to hire for Reels](/en/blog/quien-contratar-para-reels-medellin).

## Decision table

| You need… | Hire… |
|-----------|-------|
| Reply to DMs and publish approved work | Community manager |
| Calendar + vendors + reporting | Social media manager |
| What to say and why | Strategist |
| Reels/TikTok volume | Editor or UGC creator |
