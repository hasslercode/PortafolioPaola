## Short answer

Colombia searches heavily for TikTok video and marketing terms, but businesses need **native format + testing + measurement**, then **TikTok Ads**.

Combine organic posts with [UGC for brands](/en/services/ugc-brand-videos) and a clear offer. Paola Hoyos helps design the system and produce assets you can scale with paid.

## Organic first

| Format | When | Success signal |
|--------|------|----------------|
| Hook + tip | Fast authority | Above-average retention |
| Demo / unboxing | Physical product or app | Saves, shares |
| UGC testimonial | Trust and future ads | Intent comments |
| Adapted trend | Reach | Profile traffic, not views only |
| Behind the scenes | Human brand | Followers aligned to buyer |

See [hooks](/en/blog/hooks-primeros-3-segundos-tiktok), [brand videos IG/TikTok](/en/blog/videos-para-marcas-instagram-tiktok), [organic vs paid UGC](/en/blog/ugc-organico-vs-pagado).

## TikTok Ads without wasting budget

Pick 2–3 creatives with best organic retention, set a clear objective, test UGC-style vs branded, refresh every 7–14 days, and report with [UGC campaign metrics](/en/blog/metricas-para-campanas-ugc).

## Who to hire

UGC creator, strategist + editor, or performance agency once creatives and budget are stable. See [who to hire for Reels Medellín](/en/blog/quien-contratar-para-reels-medellin) and [how to hire a UGC creator](/en/blog/como-contratar-creador-ugc).
