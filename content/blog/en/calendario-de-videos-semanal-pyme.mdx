## Short answer

A **weekly video calendar for SMEs** turns video from "Tuesday urgency" into predictable system. In Colombia, brands that grow organically publish regularly — not when "there is time".

This guide offers weekly template, monthly rhythm, minimum roles and KPIs to operate without burning out the founder.

## Why weekly, not just monthly

Monthly plan gives direction; weekly plan gives execution. Week is natural unit for:

- Batch filming
- Freelancer edit delivery
- Publishing + comment response
- Seven-day metric adjustment

Without weekly breakdown, the month evaporates in a rushed week four.

## Base weekly template (2–3 videos)

| Day | Format | Objective | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | Educational / myth | Consideration | "3 mistakes choosing ___" |
| Wednesday | Demo / product | Conversion | "How it works in 30s" |
| Friday | Social proof / UGC | Trust | Client testimonial |
| (Optional Saturday) | Promo / retail | Sale | Weekend offer |

Adjust days to real traffic in insights. Food may publish Thu–Sun; B2B Tue–Thu.

## Aggressive weekly template (4–5 videos)

Monday educational, Tuesday BTS, Wednesday demo, Thursday adapted trend, Friday testimonial, Saturday geo promo. Requires bi-weekly four-to-six-hour batch or outsourced [monthly pack](/en/blog/pack-de-contenido-mensual-cuantos-videos).

## Integrated monthly rhythm

**Week 1 — Planning:** review metrics, pick 8–12 topics aligned with [content strategy](/en/blog/estrategia-de-contenido-colombia), write hooks + briefs ([editor brief](/en/blog/como-briefear-a-tu-editor-de-video))

**Week 2 — Batch filming:** one to two sessions, four to eight scripts, generic brand B-roll

**Week 3 — Edit + QA:** editor delivery, mobile caption/CTA review, schedule posts

**Week 4 — Publish + optimize:** respond comments under 24 h, one to two reactive pieces, document top performer

## Content pillars (rotation)

1. **Educate** — tips, myths, how-to
2. **Demonstrate** — product/service in action
3. **Prove** — testimonials, cases, numbers
4. **Humanize** — BTS, team, founder
5. **Sell** — offer, real urgency, retail

Each week touch at least three different pillars.

## Multi-platform calendar

Monday publish Reels; Tuesday TikTok hook variant; Wednesday Shorts repurpose; Friday Reels + TikTok. See [Reels vs TikTok vs Shorts](/en/blog/reels-vs-tiktok-vs-shorts-colombia). Do not triple load without planned variants.

## Minimum SME roles

| Role | Hours/week | Can be |
|---|---|---|
| Strategist / director | 2 h | Founder |
| Filming | 2–4 h batch | Founder / CM |
| Editing | 4–8 h | Freelance / [production](/en/services/content-production) |
| Publishing + community | 3 h | Internal CM |

Solo founder: max two videos/week sustainable — prioritize hook quality.

## Calendar tools

Notion/Airtable/Sheets for planning; Meta Business Suite for IG/FB scheduling; TikTok mostly manual OK. Fields: date, pillar, hook, status, published link, seven-day KPI.

## Suggested weekly time blocks

Monday 9am forty-five minutes: metrics + confirm week topics. Wednesday thirty minutes: QA ready videos + schedule. Friday thirty minutes: comments + learnings. Bi-weekly four hours: batch filming. Protect blocks as CEO meeting — non-negotiable.

## Weekly KPIs

Videos published vs planned (target ≥90%), average retention, total saves + shares, attributable WA/DM messages, top video of week → document hook. Cross [organic ROI](/en/blog/roi-contenido-organico).

## Contingency: impossible week

Publish one pre-edited evergreen from bank; repurpose ninety-day top video with new hook; authentic story "short week this week"; do not disappear fourteen days. Bank of four evergreen videos prevents gaps.

## Quick ideas when calendar is empty

Real client question → answer video; recurring comment → myth bust; Monday BTS process; honest product comparison; "one mistake we made and learned"

## Physical retail integration

Monday product of week; Wednesday in-store client (permission); Friday weekend promo + geo tag; daily stories queue, shelf news. Digital drives foot traffic.

## Calendar mistakes

Planning without batch; twelve ideas zero scripts; ignoring edit in timeline; publishing without comment response; no trend slot; same formats eight weeks straight.

## Scale with outsourcing

Signals for [content production](/en/services/content-production) or [monthly management](/en/services/monthly-management): calendar compliance under seventy percent three months; OK filming retention, bad edit; founder one hundred percent video ops without selling. Outsource edit first; strategy if ideation fails.

## Summary

A **weekly video calendar for SMEs** with 2–4 posts, rotating pillars, bi-weekly batch and Friday metric review turns video into repeatable operation. Plan week by week inside monthly rhythm; protect time blocks; maintain evergreen bank.

Consistency wins Colombian algorithms more than accidental viral.
