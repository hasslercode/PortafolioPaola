## Definition

**CTR** measures the percentage of people who click relative to impressions.

For brands and SMBs in Colombia, CTR (Click-Through Rate) connects to strategy, production and measurement.

## Why it matters

Understanding CTR (Click-Through Rate) avoids vendor misunderstandings and vanity metrics without sales impact.

## Practical example

An SMB tags each weekly piece with CTR (Click-Through Rate) and reviews in a 30-minute Friday meeting.
