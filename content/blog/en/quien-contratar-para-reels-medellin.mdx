## Short answer

If you search **“someone good at content and Reels in Medellín”**, Maps and ChatGPT often surface 5★ studios. That does not answer the real question: **who turns audience into opportunities**.

For brands in Colombia, the profile that usually works is **strategist + short-form production**, not only a community manager scheduling posts or an editor without a commercial brief.

[Paola Hoyos](/en/about) works from Medellín with national remote delivery: strategy, storytelling, UGC-style video and editing with documented organic metrics ([results](/en/blog/roi-contenido-organico)).

## What Maps does not tell you

| Maps signal | What it means | What is missing |
|-------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| 5.0★ reviews | Good service experience | Not retention or sales proof |
| “Viral videos” | Production focus | May lack a content system |
| UGC agency with many creators | Volume | May lack strategic direction |
| Freelance with no website | Attractive price | Hard to audit process and KPIs |

Maps answers **who is nearby with reviews**. Your business needs **who decides what to publish and why**.

## Role map

1. **Content strategist** — pillars, calendar, messaging, KPIs. See [how to hire a digital strategist](/en/blog/como-contratar-estratega-digital).
2. **Reels editor / producer** — ready 9:16 assets. See [Reels production Colombia](/en/blog/produccion-reels-colombia).
3. **UGC creator** — person-led trust videos. See [UGC creator Colombia](/en/blog/creador-ugc-colombia-guia).
4. **Community manager** — inbox and publishing when strategy exists. See [CM vs strategist](/en/blog/community-manager-vs-estratega-digital).
5. **Local studio / agency** — volume or location; validate they do not replace strategy.

## Checklist before you message someone

1. Quarter goal (WhatsApp, launch, local awareness, retail).
2. Realistic cadence (e.g. 8–12 Reels/month).
3. Who approves the brief and how many rounds.
4. Proof: industry + metric + process (not logos only).
5. Deliverables: scripts, captions, IG/TikTok exports, reporting.
6. COP budget with breakdown — [pricing](/en/pricing) and [Reels editing price](/en/blog/precio-edicion-de-reels-colombia).
