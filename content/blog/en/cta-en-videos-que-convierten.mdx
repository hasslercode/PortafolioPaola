## Short answer

The **video CTA** — call to action — is where content becomes business. On Colombian Reels, TikTok and Shorts, most brands lose conversion not from bad filming but from vague closings: "follow us", "link in bio" without urgency, or abrupt silence at the end.

A converting CTA names the action, immediate benefit and exact channel.

## Anatomy of an effective short-form CTA

### Specific

Bad: "Contact us"
Good: "Message WhatsApp 300… and request a quote with no commitment"

### Timed

Appears when the viewer already believes the promise — after proof, demo or testimonial.

### Multi-channel in frame

Voice + on-screen text + gesture (point down, show phone)

### One action

One video = one measurable objective

### Measurable

You can count "WANT" comments, WA messages, UTM clicks or store visits with code

## CTAs by commercial objective

| Objective | CTA example | Metric |
|---|---|---|
| Service leads | "Comment AUDIT and I will DM you" | DMs / comments |
| WA sale | "WhatsApp link in bio — spot today" | Chats started |
| Ecommerce | "Use code REEL10 at checkout" | Attributed sales |
| Awareness | "Follow for part 2 tomorrow" | Follows + series retention |
| Education | "Save this for your next purchase" | Saves |
| Local retail | "Mention this video in store ___" | Redemptions |

Align CTA with [content strategy](/en/blog/estrategia-de-contenido-colombia).

## Timing: when NOT to ask

**Timing mistakes:**

- CTA at second 2 before the hook
- Two competing CTAs mid and end
- Video ends without CTA because "caption says it"

Caption reinforces; video closes. Assume seventy percent watch without reading full caption.

**Practical rule:** Value seventy percent of video, CTA final thirty percent. On thirty-second pieces, CTA from second 22.

## CTA copy formulas that work

### Keyword in comments

"Comment [WORD] and I will send [free resource]"
Works because it triggers algorithmic engagement plus a public lead list.

### Real urgency

"Only 15 spots this week in Medellín" — only if true.

### Immediate benefit

"I reply with exact price in 10 minutes"

### Social proof + CTA

"200+ clients in Bogotá already use this — write INFO"

### Micro-commitment

"Save if you will renovate the bathroom this year" — low friction, high saves.

## Visual CTA design in editing

Professional [social video editing](/en/blog/edicion-de-videos-para-redes-colombia) includes:

- Large centered text or lower third last 5 s
- Contrast color (yellow on dark, white on video)
- WA / cart / arrow icon if applicable
- Subtle entrance animation — not aggressive blink
- Safe area: text not covered by IG/TikTok UI

Brief editor with **exact** CTA copy, desired font and whether logo appears at close.

## Verbal CTA: Colombia tone

Speak conversationally, not radio announcer:

- "If you are in Cali and into this, message me"
- "Save this so you know which one to pick at the store"
- "I read every comment — replying today"

Avoid excessive formality and forced slang that does not match your brand.

## CTAs by platform

Same video, light adaptation:

**Instagram Reels:** "Link in bio" + story sticker with direct link

**TikTok:** "Link in bio" or pinned comment with instructions

**YouTube Shorts:** "Description below" + pinned comment

See [export video Instagram TikTok](/en/blog/exportar-video-para-instagram-y-tiktok) for technical variants.

## Storytelling + CTA: do not break the spell

If you use emotional narrative ([short-form storytelling](/en/blog/storytelling-en-video-corto)), CTA should flow:

"If this story sounds like yours, comment TELLME and I will explain how we solved it"

Emotional bridge → action. Do not cut from tears to "BUY NOW" without transition.

## CTA mistakes that kill conversion

1. **Generic** — "Follow for more content"
2. **Invisible** — Mentioned quickly without text
3. **Fake urgency** — "Last day" every day
4. **High friction** — Twelve-field form from Reel
5. **Caption mismatch** — Video says WA, caption says web
6. **No response** — Ask for comments, silence 48 h
7. **Forgotten in edit** — Export without close; silent death

## A/B testing CTAs simply

Same video body, film two closings:

- Close A: keyword comment
- Close B: direct WhatsApp

Publish alternate weeks or split paid audience. Measure qualified messages, not just clicks.

## Paid vs organic CTA

**Organic:** micro-commitment (save, follow, comment) builds community

**Paid:** direct action (buy, register) with optimized landing

Reuse organic winners in ads with more aggressive CTA in last 3 s — re-edit close, do not reshoot all.

## WhatsApp funnel integration

For Colombian SMEs, WA is the #1 post-video channel:

1. Video CTA: "Write HELLO to 300…"
2. Auto or human reply with three qualification questions
3. Follow-up within 24 h

Video opens conversation; commercial close lives in chat.

## Pre-publish CTA checklist

- [ ] One clear action
- [ ] Identical copy in voice and on-screen text
- [ ] Caption reinforces CTA
- [ ] Link / WA works
- [ ] Team responds within 2 h
- [ ] Metric defined (comments X, chats Y)
- [ ] Safe area verified on mobile

## Summary

A **video CTA that converts** is specific, timely, visible and measurable. Stop closing with "follow us" if your goal is sales. Name action, channel and benefit in voice and screen. Test closings, respond fast and align editor + caption + sales team.

Video brings attention; CTA brings business.
