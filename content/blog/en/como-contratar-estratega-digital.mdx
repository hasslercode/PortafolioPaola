## Short answer

Hiring a digital strategist in Colombia is not “finding someone who posts pretty”. It is choosing the person who turns **business goal → content system → metrics**, and knows when to produce, edit or not publish.

If you need a remote content strategist with retail/consumer proof, see [content strategist Colombia remote](/en/blog/content-strategist-colombia-remoto) and [About — recruiters](/en/about#reclutadores).

## Checklist before you hire

1. Explicit business goal
2. Measurable KPIs (intent messages, link CTR, saves, Reel retention)
3. Real case evidence
4. Clear deliverables and timelines
5. Decision channel (who approves briefs)

## Strategist vs community vs UGC creator

| Role | Main delivery | When to hire |
|------|---------------|--------------|
| Digital / content strategist | Pillars, calendar, messaging | When you post without a system |
| Community manager | Publishing, inbox, community | When strategy already exists |
| UGC / Reel creator-editor | Ready-to-publish assets | When production is the bottleneck |

## Red flags

Follower promises without strategy. Generic 12-post packs. No Insights access. Logo walls without transparency.
