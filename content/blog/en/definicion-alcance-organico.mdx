## Definition

**Organic reach** is people who see your content without paid advertising.

For brands and SMBs in Colombia, Organic reach connects to strategy, production and measurement.

## Why it matters

Understanding Organic reach avoids vendor misunderstandings and vanity metrics without sales impact.

## Practical example

An SMB tags each weekly piece with Organic reach and reviews in a 30-minute Friday meeting.
