## Short answer

**Home video lighting** is the cheapest quality multiplier. An entrepreneur in Medellín, Bogotá or Cali can record professional-looking Reels using side window + one LED, tripod and [phone recording](/en/blog/grabacion-profesional-con-celular) — no rented studio.

Scale sets and editing via [content production](/en/services/content-production) and [pricing](/en/pricing).

## Why light matters more than camera

Badly lit skin, harsh under-eye shadows and noisy dark areas scream “homemade”. Audience associates clean light with trust — critical in [sales scripts](/en/blog/guion-para-reels-de-ventas).

Consumer brands invest in consistent light in activations. Campaigns with **+1.3M organic views** (Coca-Cola, Starbucks, H&M, TOTTO, Cine Colombia in Parque Alegra contexts) depend on system, not luck.

## Setup 1: window only (zero budget)

- Side window, not behind subject
- White diffuse curtain
- White reflector (foam board) opposite
- Same hour batch recording

Limitation: clouds and hour change color between days.

## Setup 2: one LED + window

- Bicolor LED 45° to face ~70%
- Window as soft fill opposite
- Camera slightly offset between light and subject

Works in typical Colombian apartments with limited depth.

## Setup 3: simplified three-point

| Light | Position | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Key | 45° right, eye height | Models face |
| Fill | 45° left, weaker | Opens shadows |
| Hair/back | Behind lateral | Separates from background |

At home, hair light can be lamp bounced off wall.

## Color temperature

Balance all sources ~5500K or convert all to tungsten for intentional warm look. Unchecked mix = sick skin on camera.

## Backgrounds at home

- Plain neutral wall
- Curated shelf (3 brand objects)
- Green screen only if keying well — else plain wall wins

Vertical [9:16](/en/blog/formato-9-16-guia-emprendedores) shows more ceiling/floor — clean visible zone.

## Ring light: when yes and no

**Yes:** beauty, makeup, close face with diffusion.

**Not ideal:** flat corporate look unmodified; visible ring in eyes distracts.

Prefer small softbox panel if budget allows.

## Avoid ceiling light

Downlights create harsh under-eye shadows. Turn off overhead tungsten; use face-height LEDs.

## Person vs product

**Person:** soft key, catchlight in eyes.

**Product:** raking side light for texture; controlled background; avoid shiny packaging reflections.

Alternate shots same set for [retention editing](/en/blog/como-editar-reels-que-retienen).

## Color in post

Good light reduces extreme grade. Light exposure and balance pass in DaVinci/CapCut. [Premium editing](/en/blog/diferencia-entre-edicion-basica-y-premium) uniformizes monthly series.

## Captions and light

High face/background contrast helps [caption](/en/blog/subtitulos-en-reels-mejores-practicas) legibility.

## Common mistakes

1. Window behind (silhouette)
2. Window + tungsten unfiltered mix
3. LED at 100% blue
4. Background brighter than subject
5. Setup change between batch takes
6. Cheap flickering LED on camera

## Lighting checklist

- [ ] No hard backlight
- [ ] Catchlight in eyes
- [ ] Consistent temperature
- [ ] Intentional background
- [ ] 10s camera test before batch
- [ ] Same setup for batch pieces

## Window light by time of day

Early morning: cool soft light — ideal for skin. Midday: harsh — avoid direct. Golden hour: warm flattering — creator favorite in Colombia. Overcast: natural softbox — excellent for batch.

## DIY modifiers

White curtain = diffuser. Foam board = reflector. White sheet = bounce. Black cardboard = flag. No Amazon studio required to start.

## Lighting for diverse skin tones

Avoid underexposure losing detail. Soft front key; do not trust auto brightness without test. Manual exposure slightly right on histogram without clipping highlights.

## RGB accent trends

RGB background OK for tech/gaming. Neutral light signals credibility for consulting or health content.

## Before/after lighting demo

Record 10s same script with ceiling only vs window+LED setup. Comparison clip retains as educational meta-content.

## Advanced checklist

No banding flicker on camera. Visible catchlight in eyes. Background does not compete with subject. Uniform temperature in batch. 10s test before 30 min session.
