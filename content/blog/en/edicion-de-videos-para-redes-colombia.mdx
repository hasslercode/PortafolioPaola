## Short answer

If you search for **social video editing** in Colombia, the goal is not “pretty cuts”. The goal is retention past three seconds, silent comprehension via captions, and a clear CTA.

## What a professional edit must include

1. Hook in 1–3 seconds
2. Retention pacing
3. Mobile-readable captions
4. Consistent visual system
5. Explicit CTA
6. Correct 9:16 export
