## Short answer

**Hooks first 3 seconds TikTok** (and Reels) are a contract with the algorithm: “stay and I'll give value”. Without clear promise on frame one, the rest of the script statistically does not exist.

Scale hooks + editing via [content production](/en/services/content-production). [Pricing](/en/pricing) for monthly volume.

## Why 3 seconds is not arbitrary

Platforms calculate early retention to decide next impressions. Sharp drop at second 2–3 = swipe-away signal.

Consumer brands — Coca-Cola, Starbucks, H&M, TOTTO, Cine Colombia — open activations with immediate visual impact. Portfolios with **+1.3M organic views** in 90 days (documented retail contexts) do not waste opening on slow logo.

## Perfect hook anatomy

1. **Visual:** movement, expressive face, product in action, zoom-in
2. **Verbal:** one short phrase, no “hi everyone”
3. **On-screen text:** legible reinforcement in [9:16](/en/blog/formato-9-16-guia-emprendedores)
4. **Caption:** synced from word one — [best practices](/en/blog/subtitulos-en-reels-mejores-practicas)
5. **Audio:** strong voice or punctual SFX, [low music](/en/blog/musica-libre-de-derechos-reels-colombia)

## 12 tested hook templates

1. “Stop ___ if you want ___”
2. “Why doesn't anyone tell you ___?”
3. “In 30 seconds I'll show you ___”
4. “3 mistakes when ___”
5. “This cost me ___ — learn free”
6. Immediate demo without intro
7. Before/after split frame
8. Unexpected object in hand
9. “POV: you're ___ in Medellín/Bogotá…”
10. Soft sector controversy
11. Specific number + result
12. Client testimonial frame 1 (with permission)

Adapt to [sales script](/en/blog/guion-para-reels-de-ventas) or education.

## Visual pattern interrupt

- Cold jump cut from black
- Whip pan
- Giant text filling screen
- Hard location cut
- 0.5s slow motion then normal speed

[Edit retaining Reels](/en/blog/como-editar-reels-que-retienen) details post-hook rhythm.

## Hook mistakes

1. 3+ second animated logo
2. “Hi, welcome to my channel”
3. Loud music before voice
4. Small illegible text
5. Generic hook (“marketing tips”)
6. False promise not delivered
7. Same hook as 10 competitors verbatim

## Recording: batch hooks

Record 5 different hooks before long bodies. High energy at session start — [phone recording](/en/blog/grabacion-profesional-con-celular) on tripod.

Consistent [lighting](/en/blog/iluminacion-para-videos-en-casa) so A/B compares message, not exposure.

## Editing first 3 seconds

- Enter on frame 0, not slow fade from black
- Remove pre-speech breaths
- 105% zoom on keyword
- Subtle SFX on cut (not caricature B2B)

## Scroll-stop psychology

Brain evaluates novelty in milliseconds. Movement, contrast, human face and large text trigger attention. Feed repetition desensitizes — hook must break pattern without confusing.

## Hooks by Colombia industry

Restaurants: steam, cheese stretch, first bite ASMR. Real estate: before/after remodel, 3s POV tour. Fashion: outfit transformation hard cut. Adapt templates to [sales script](/en/blog/guion-para-reels-de-ventas).

## 3-frame hook storyboard

Frame 1: problem situation visual. Frame 2: large question text. Frame 3: facial reaction or product entry. Storyboard in notes before recording saves takes.

## Hook fatigue

Rotating hook category monthly while keeping core promise prevents audience habituation.
