## Short answer

The **difference between basic and premium editing** shows in consistency: every Reel feels part of the same brand, retains past second three and does not embarrass in feed next to polished competitors.

Compare packs on [content production](/en/services/content-production) and ranges on [pricing](/en/pricing).

## Why it matters in Colombia 2026

Vertical feed is saturated. Founders edit in CapCut; retail brands invest in postproduction. Documented campaigns — Coca-Cola, Starbucks, H&M, TOTTO, Cine Colombia — within ecosystems like Parque Alegra exceeded **+1.3M organic views** in 90 days with **system**, not a single template.

Premium does not copy corporate: it adapts discipline to SME budget.

## Basic editing: definition

**Typically includes:**
- Rough cut + dead air removal
- Jump cuts
- Corrected auto captions
- Licensed stock/trend music
- 1080×1920 export

**Tools:** CapCut, InShot, mobile editor.

**Ideal for:** founder starting, 2–4 pieces/month, tight budget.

See base in [how to edit retaining Reels](/en/blog/como-editar-reels-que-retienen).

## Premium editing: definition

**Typically includes:**
- Retention analysis per piece
- Frame-by-frame hook edits
- Brand-designed captions — [best practices](/en/blog/subtitulos-en-reels-mejores-practicas)
- Motion lower thirds, selective kinetic typography
- LUT/match color grade across pieces
- Sound design and ~-14 LUFS loudness
- [9:16](/en/blog/formato-9-16-guia-emprendedores) safe area QA
- Professional [music](/en/blog/musica-libre-de-derechos-reels-colombia) mix

**Tools:** Premiere, DaVinci, selective After Effects.

**Ideal for:** 8+ monthly pieces, visual ecommerce, high-ticket services, retail.

## Comparison table

| Criterion | Basic | Premium |
|---|---|---|
| Time per 30s piece | 30–90 min | 2–5 h |
| Visual system | Ad hoc | Brand manual |
| Motion | Generic templates | Recurring custom |
| Color | Auto | Consistent grade |
| Hook retention | Luck-dependent | Intentional edit |
| Monthly volume | 1–4 OK | 8–20 scalable |
| Relative cost | Low | Medium-high |

## Signs you need premium

1. Retention always drops at second 2–3 despite good script
2. Feed looks inconsistent piece to piece
3. Direct competitors look more polished
4. Internal team spends 15+ h/week on timeline
5. Ads amplify pieces — errors magnify
6. Physical retail brand with multiple SKUs

## Signs basic is enough (for now)

1. Low volume, strong founder-led message
2. Audience values raw authentic (some B2B)
3. Limited budget — better invest in [lighting](/en/blog/iluminacion-para-videos-en-casa) and [audio](/en/blog/grabacion-profesional-con-celular)
4. Initial niche testing

## What premium is NOT

- Free transitions every 2 seconds
- Extreme LUT darkening product
- Motion distracting from CTA
- 20 revisions without brief

Premium serves message — [sales script](/en/blog/guion-para-reels-de-ventas) first.

## Hybrid workflow

Months 1–2: basic + learn retaining formats.

Month 3+: premium on winning formats; basic on fast trend reacts.

Outsource premium via [content production](/en/services/content-production).

## Brief for premium editor

- Piece objective and KPI (retention, DMs, clicks)
- Brand manual (fonts, colors)
- 2–3 reference links
- Tagged rushes
- Preferred [hooks](/en/blog/hooks-primeros-3-segundos-tiktok) per take
- Revision limit

## Expected ROI (no invented numbers)

Premium should improve:
- Watch time vs your previous pieces
- Brand perception (DMs “looks pro”)
- Publishing speed (hours returned to founder)

Does not promise fixed views per piece.

## Hiring mistakes

1. Choose lowest price only
2. No strategy shared — editor cuts blind
3. Request premium look without usable material
4. Mix 3 different editor styles same month
5. Do not measure retention post-delivery

## Decision checklist

- [ ] Monthly volume defined
- [ ] Material shot to minimum standard
- [ ] Scripts or bullets exist
- [ ] Budget aligned to [pricing](/en/pricing)
- [ ] Baseline KPI measured (current retention)

## When basic won

Authentic founder in technical niche posting raw tips — audience values speed and truth over motion. Premium would add friction without ROI.

## When premium paid

Fashion ecommerce 12 Reels/month: uniform grade + kinetic type — measure your own relative retention improvement over 90 days.

## Deliverables compared

Basic: final MP4, 1 revision, partial brand kit. Premium: brand kit applied, 2–3 revisions, optional retention report, sometimes project file.

## Migration path basic → premium

Month 1: premium first 5s hooks only. Month 2: premium hooks + color. Month 3: full premium on winning formats.
