## Short answer

**Exporting video for Instagram and TikTok** correctly avoids the frustrating scenario: hours of editing that look pixelated, have black bars or quiet audio when published. Platforms recompress everything — your job is delivering a clean master that survives compression.

This guide consolidates 2026 specs, practical presets and frequent technical mistakes in Colombian production.

## Recommended master specs 2026

| Parameter | Reels / TikTok | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Aspect ratio | 9:16 | Native vertical |
| Resolution | 1080 × 1920 | Professional minimum |
| Video codec | H.264 | Universal compatibility |
| Profile | High | Better compression |
| Frame rate | 30 fps | 60 fps if action |
| Video bitrate | 15–25 Mbps 1080p | Raise if heavy motion |
| Audio codec | AAC | Stereo 48 kHz |
| Audio loudness | -14 LUFS integrated | Streaming orientation |
| Duration | 15–90 s typical | By message |
| Color | Rec.709 | Web standard |

## Safe areas: where to place text

App UI covers edges:

- **Top 10–15%:** username, trending music, search
- **Bottom 20–25%:** caption, buttons, description
- **Central safe zone:** main text and key faces

Preview on iPhone SE and Pro Max — different sizes.

## Professional export workflow

1. Edit timeline at 1080×1920 project resolution
2. Color grade Rec.709
3. Audio mix clear voice, music ducking
4. Burn captions or export separate SRT
5. Export high bitrate H.264 master MP4
6. Mobile QA before upload
7. Platform hook/caption variants if applicable
8. Archive project + master + SRT

## Tool presets

### CapCut

1080p, 30 fps, high quality or custom elevated bitrate. Avoid WhatsApp re-export.

### Adobe Premiere Pro

H.264 Match Source – High bitrate or custom 1080×1920, VBR 2 pass, ~20 Mbps target, AAC 320 kbps audio.

### DaVinci Resolve

Custom MP4 H.264, 1080×1920, 30fps, ~20000 Kb/s; normalize loudness in Fairlight.

Document internal preset for batch consistency.

## Instagram Reels vs TikTok export differences

| Factor | Instagram Reels | TikTok |
|---|---|---|
| Cover thumbnail | Critical profile CTR | Less weight |
| Native app text | IG stickers | TikTok built-in text |
| Recompression | Aggressive feed | Aggressive; variable sharpness |

**Practice:** Single master → native upload each app beats cross-watermark repost.

## Audio normalization

- Integrated **-14 LUFS** (orientation, not dogma)
- Peak max -1 dBTP avoids clipping
- Music 18–22 dB under voice when speaking
- Use WAV on timeline; avoid generational MP3

Bad audio survives less than mediocre video.

## Bitrate: why it matters

Five Mbps 1080p vertical export falls apart post-upload. Motion, fine text and grain worsen.

**Rule:** When in doubt, raise bitrate. Storage cost is minimal vs reshooting.

## Common export mistakes

1. 16:9 timeline with late crop — loses composition
2. 720 → 1080 upscale — false sharpness
3. WhatsApp re-export — destroys quality
4. Phone variable frame rate without fix — audio desync
5. HDR without SDR conversion — washed web colors
6. Captions outside safe zone — covered after upload UI
7. Exact 90:00.001 duration — rare glitches; leave margin

## Paid export (Meta Ads / TikTok Ads)

Same 9:16 master; variant without copyright music if needed; hook first 3 s silent-first; conservative safe area for ad CTA buttons.
