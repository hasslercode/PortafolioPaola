## Short answer

The **9:16 format guide for entrepreneurs** matters because vertical is the native language of the 2026 feed. Reels, TikTok and Shorts consume full mobile screen. Shooting, editing and exporting vertical from the start avoids ugly crops and maximizes retention.

Scale production with [content production](/en/services/content-production). Check [pricing](/en/pricing) for monthly packs.

## Why 9:16 is non-negotiable

Vertical scroll uses 100% viewport. Horizontal video with black bars or aggressive zoom feels amateur and reduces usable space for hook, captions and product.

Consumer brands in Colombia — Coca-Cola, Starbucks, H&M, TOTTO, Cine Colombia — produce vertical masters for retail activations. Ecosystems like Parque Alegra accumulated **+1.3M organic views** in 90 days when format, cadence and message align.

## Recommended technical specs

| Parameter | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Aspect ratio | 9:16 |
| Resolution | 1080 × 1920 |
| Frame rate | 30 fps (60 if action) |
| Codec | H.264 |
| Audio | AAC, normalized |

## Native vertical vs crop

**Native vertical:** frame face, product and text for height. Use tripod. Set phone camera to 9:16 before recording.

**Horizontal crop:** last resort. Reframe with keyframes following subject.

Deep-dive capture in [professional phone recording](/en/blog/grabacion-profesional-con-celular).

## Safe areas by platform

**Instagram Reels:** buttons right, caption bottom, username top.

**TikTok:** similar UI with slight device differences.

**Rule:** no critical text in bottom 15% or narrow right strip.

Related: [Reels captions best practices](/en/blog/subtitulos-en-reels-mejores-practicas).

## Composition in vertical

**Talking head:** eyes in upper third, caption space lower.

**Product:** centered close-ups.

**On-screen text:** large, few words, high contrast. See [3-second hooks](/en/blog/hooks-primeros-3-segundos-tiktok).

## Export from popular editors

**CapCut:** 9:16 ratio, 1080p, 30fps.

**DaVinci / Premiere:** 1080×1920 timeline from new project.

**Mistake:** export 16:9 and upload to Reels trusting the app.

## Vertical history and 2026 context

Mobile consumption migrated from horizontal YouTube to vertical Stories, TikTok and Reels. Entrepreneurs still exporting 16:9 "because cinematic" compete with fraction of usable screen. In Colombia, high smartphone penetration makes 9:16 cultural default.

## Common devices in Colombia

Mid-range Android and iPhone 11–15 dominate. Preview 1080×1920 on at least one non-flagship Android — aggressive compression on some models.

## Related aspect ratios

**1:1** legacy square feed crop. **4:5** Instagram portrait feed, not fullscreen Reels. **16:9** horizontal YouTube. **9:16** native Reels/TikTok/Shorts.

## Multi-camera vertical

Two portrait angles (wide + close) alternated in edit increase retention. Both cameras must shoot portrait or careful reframe.

## Export troubleshooting

**Blurry:** low bitrate or double compression. **Black bars:** wrong timeline ratio. **Desync audio:** variable frame rate — lock 30fps. **Washed colors:** bad HDR mapping — disable HDR if inconsistent.
