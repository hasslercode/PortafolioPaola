## Short answer

In Colombia, searches for **social media management** outpace pure “content strategy”. Many buyers ask for ops, but businesses usually need a **system + retaining short-form assets**.

Good monthly management connects calendar, Reels/TikTok, community and reporting — not just “30 posts”.

## Checklist

1. Quarter goal
2. Pillars and buyer persona
3. Realistic cadence (2–4 Reels/week often beats 30 weak posts)
4. Production or briefs to editor/UGC
5. Publishing + inbox
6. KPI reporting (retention, messages, clicks)
7. Feedback rounds and file ownership

## Roles people confuse

| You need… | Role |
|-----------|------|
| Inbox + publishing | Community manager |
| Channel + vendor coordination | Social media manager |
| Messaging and prioritization | Strategist |
| Short-form volume | Editor / UGC creator |
