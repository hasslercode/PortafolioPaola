## Short answer

In Colombia, **Instagram Reels** draws huge search interest, but much of it is generic (downloaders, converters). For a **business**, ask: **what Reels to publish, with what structure, and what to measure**.

Reels that retain attention plus a WhatsApp/bio CTA convert better than pretty video without a message. For production with a system, see [Reels production Colombia](/en/blog/produccion-reels-colombia) or [content production](/en/services/content-production).

## Feed structure that works

1. **Hook 0–3 s** — [hooks guide](/en/blog/hooks-primeros-3-segundos-tiktok).
2. **Body** — one point (demo, tip, social proof, behind the scenes).
3. **Proof** — result, number, testimonial or product in use.
4. **CTA** — comment keyword, DM, bio link, visit.
5. **Captions** — [Reels subtitles](/en/blog/subtitulos-en-reels-mejores-practicas).

Editing deep dive: [how to edit Reels that retain](/en/blog/como-editar-reels-que-retienen).

## Topics that move business

| Reel type | Goal | KPI |

| Product/service demo | Consideration | Saves, shares |
| Social proof / UGC | Trust | Comments, DMs |
| Offer / launch | Conversion | Bio clicks, WhatsApp |

## Realistic cadence for Colombian SMBs

Week 1: one pilot Reel + insights. Weeks 2–4: 2–4 Reels/week in batch. Day 30: double winning formats. Day 90: decide retainer vs internal team.
