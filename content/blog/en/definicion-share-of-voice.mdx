## Definition

**Share of voice** measures how much your brand speaks in a topic vs competitors.

For brands and SMBs in Colombia, Share of voice connects to strategy, production and measurement.

## Why it matters

Understanding Share of voice avoids vendor misunderstandings and vanity metrics without sales impact.

An SMB tags each weekly piece with Share of voice and reviews in a 30-minute Friday meeting.
