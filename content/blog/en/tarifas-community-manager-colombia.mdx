## 2026 orientation ranges
| Focus | Monthly range (COP) | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Presence + community | $2,000,000 – $3,500,000 | Personal brands and businesses that already know what to say |
| Organic growth | $3,500,000 – $5,500,000 | SMBs with conversion goals and recurring video |
| Strategy + premium ops | $5,500,000 – $9,000,000+ | Retail / multi-brand / high volume |

Fees under ~$1,500,000 COP/month in Colombia are usually **scheduling only** (templates, little research, weak editing). If you need Reel retention, commercial judgment and useful reporting, budget in the ranges above — or quote [premium monthly management](/en/services/monthly-management).

## How to decide
Don’t compare price alone: compare **service scope**, social proof and KPI clarity.
