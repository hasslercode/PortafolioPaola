## Short answer

**Briefing your video editor** translates commercial strategy into editable instructions. The most expensive mistake Colombian SMEs make is not paying for editing — it is paying for three rounds of "not what I imagined" because the brief was three WhatsApp lines.

A solid brief saves time, money and freelancer relationships.

## Why the brief matters more than editor software

Premiere, DaVinci or CapCut are tools. The editor decides:

- Rhythm and retention
- Visual message hierarchy
- Captions and mobile legibility
- Music and audio balance
- CTA timing and design

Without direction, they apply personal taste — excellent for another brand type, not yours.

## Perfect brief anatomy

### 1. Commercial context

- Project / campaign name
- Objective: awareness / consideration / conversion
- Specific product or service
- Audience (city, age, pain)
- Primary platform (IG, TikTok, both)

### 2. Narrative structure

- Written hook (exact first 3 s text)
- Full script or timestamp bullets
- Proof included (testimonial, data, demo)
- Exact CTA (word for word)

See [video CTAs](/en/blog/cta-en-videos-que-convierten) and [storytelling](/en/blog/storytelling-en-video-corto).

### 3. References

- 2–3 links with **desired rhythm** (not just topic)
- Note: "I like A's pace, B's captions"
- Forbidden references ("no glitch transitions")

### 4. Technical specs

- Target final duration
- 9:16 ratio
- Burned captions / separate SRT
- Animated logo in/out yes/no
- Music: upbeat / neutral / none / trending
- Export: resolution, fps

Detail in [export video Instagram TikTok](/en/blog/exportar-video-para-instagram-y-tiktok).

### 5. Deliverables and deadlines

- Final MP4
- Optional SRT
- Source project yes/no
- Suggested cover frame (IG)
- Delivery date
- Included revisions (number)

### 6. Assets

- Rushes in named folder
- Marked favorite takes
- Logo PNG/SVG
- Brand fonts
- Brand colors hex

## Copy-paste brief template

```
PROJECT: ___
DELIVERY DATE: ___
FINAL DURATION: ___ seconds
PLATFORM: Instagram Reels / TikTok / both

OBJECTIVE: awareness / leads / sale
AUDIENCE: ___
PRODUCT/SERVICE: ___

HOOK (0-3s): "___"
DEVELOPMENT (bullets):
- ___
- ___
PROOF: ___
FINAL CTA: "___"

RHYTHM REFERENCES:
1. [link] — I like ___
2. [link] — I like ___

MANDATORY ON-SCREEN TEXT: ___
CAPTIONS: burned / SRT / style ___
LOGO: no intro / yes outro / watermark
MUSIC: ___
EXPORT: 1080x1920, 30fps, H.264

RUSHES: [folder link]
FAVORITE TAKES: minute ___ of clip ___

INCLUDED REVISIONS: 2
APPROVAL CONTACT: ___
```

## Monthly pack brief (8–12 pieces)

Use a table:

| # | Internal title | Hook | Duration | Priority | Delivery week |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Skincare myth | "You thought ___" | 30s | High | W1 |

Common visual standard on separate sheet: fonts, colors, caption style. See [monthly content pack](/en/blog/pack-de-contenido-mensual-cuantos-videos).

## Brief mistakes that inflate cost

1. **"Make it viral"** — Not an instruction
2. **Contradictory references** — one minimal, one chaotic
3. **Script change post v1** — new scope
4. **No defined CTA** — editor invents generic close
5. **Disorganized rushes** — forty-seven unnamed clips
6. **Unrealistic deadline** — twelve videos in twenty-four hours
7. **Committee approval without single decider** — infinite revisions

## Efficient async communication

- One official channel (Notion, Google Doc, email)
- Timestamped feedback: "0:12 — bigger text"
- One approver
- Consolidate feedback in one message, not seven voice notes

## Brief + technical handoff

Also specify:

- Delivery file naming: `BRAND_Reel01_vFinal.mp4`
- Upload location (Drive, Frame.io, Dropbox)
- Who receives credentials
- If paid needs variant without music

Avoid [Reels editing mistakes](/en/blog/errores-al-editar-reels-para-negocios) from technical omission.

## When strategist vs client writes brief

| Situation | Who briefs |
|---|---|
| Client knows what to say | Client with template |
| Client films but does not structure | Strategist + client validates |
| Fully outsourced production | Agency briefs, client approves |

Without strategist, start with [content strategy Colombia](/en/blog/estrategia-de-contenido-colombia) or [strategy session](/en/services/strategy-session).

## UGC vs brand material brief

**UGC:** Less edit intervention; brief asks authenticity; do not over-produce

**Brand:** Strict visual system; motion; color grade

Tell editor spectrum: "native TikTok" vs "polished Instagram retail"

## Test editor with trial brief

Before monthly pack, commission one piece with full brief. Evaluate:

- Followed literal CTA and hook?
- Rhythm vs references?
- Mobile-readable captions?
- On-time delivery?
- Consolidated feedback handled well?

Small investment avoids long contracts with creative mismatch.

**Briefing your video editor** means documenting objective, hook, script, rhythm references, specs, CTA and deadlines in one shared document. Fifteen minutes of brief saves hours of revision. Use template, table for monthly packs and one approval decider.
