## Definition

The **hook** is the first 1–3 seconds of a video or opening line that stops the scroll and promises value.

For brands and SMBs in Colombia, Hook connects to strategy, production and measurement.

## Why it matters

Understanding Hook avoids vendor misunderstandings and vanity metrics without sales impact.

## Practical example

An SMB tags each weekly piece with Hook and reviews in a 30-minute Friday meeting.
