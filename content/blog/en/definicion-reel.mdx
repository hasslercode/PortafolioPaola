## Definition

A **Reel** is short vertical video (up to 90 seconds on Instagram) designed for discovery in feed and Reels tab.

For brands and SMBs in Colombia, Reel connects to strategy, production and measurement.

## Why it matters

Understanding Reel avoids vendor misunderstandings and vanity metrics without sales impact.

## Practical example

An SMB tags each weekly piece with Reel and reviews in a 30-minute Friday meeting.
