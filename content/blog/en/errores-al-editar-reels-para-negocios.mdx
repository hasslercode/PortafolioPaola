## Short answer

Editing **Reels for business** is not adding trending music and filters. It is retention engineering: every cut, caption and audio decision affects whether someone reaches the CTA. In Colombia, SMEs invest in filming but lose distribution in post-production through avoidable mistakes.

This guide lists the fifteen most frequent failures and how to fix them before publishing.

## Mistake 1: Slow intro or long logo

**Symptom:** Retention drops forty percent in first 3 s
**Cause:** Three-to-five-second logo animation, "Hi I am…" greeting, generic drone shot
**Fix:** Immediate hook. Small logo last frame or discreet watermark

## Mistake 2: No captions or illegible ones

**Symptom:** OK reach with audio on, dead on silent feed
**Cause:** Trust in voice; auto captions size 12
**Fix:** Large captions, high contrast, central safe area. See [social video editing](/en/blog/edicion-de-videos-para-redes-colombia)

## Mistake 3: Music louder than voice

**Symptom:** Users scroll without understanding message
**Cause:** Trending track at full volume
**Fix:** Ducking: voice -6 to -12 dB above music; sidechain compression

## Mistake 4: Random jump cuts without rhythm

**Symptom:** Video feels "nervous" or confusingly amateur
**Cause:** Cutting every 0.5 s without reason
**Fix:** Cut on natural pauses, emphasis and filler removal

## Mistake 5: Excessive effects and transitions

**Symptom:** Looks like 2020 TikTok parody
**Cause:** Preset pack without criteria
**Fix:** One recurring brand effect beats ten different transitions

## Mistake 6: Text in wrong safe zone

**Symptom:** Text covered by IG/TikTok buttons
**Cause:** Export without mobile preview
**Fix:** Ten percent top and bottom margin; test on two devices

## Mistake 7: Missing CTA or caption-only

**Symptom:** Views without messages or sales
**Cause:** Forgotten in edit
**Fix:** Visual + verbal close last 5 s. See [video CTAs](/en/blog/cta-en-videos-que-convierten)

## Mistake 8: Incorrect export (bitrate, ratio)

**Symptom:** Blurry video or black bars
**Cause:** 4:5 badly cropped to 9:16; excessive compression
**Fix:** Master 1080×1920, H.264, adequate bitrate. See [export guide](/en/blog/exportar-video-para-instagram-y-tiktok)

## Mistakes 9–15 (summary)

9. **Copyright music** — muted or limited reach; use licensed libraries
10. **Inconsistent color** — one LUT per video; use brand preset
11. **Unjustified duration** — ninety seconds with thirty filler; one idea per piece
12. **Generic B-roll** — stock without connection to specific line
13. **Bad first frame** — black or blur thumbnail; design cover with hook text
14. **Unnormalized audio across pieces** — target ~-14 LUFS integrated
15. **Publishing first draft** — typos, harsh cuts, audio clip; mobile + headphone review

## Diagnosis: edit or script?

| Signal | Likely problem |
|---|---|
| Drop second 1–3 | Hook / intro edit |
| Drop second 10–15 | Slow script or no turn |
| Drop at end | Weak CTA or long video |
| Low reach overall | Hook + captions + export combo |

Do not blame editor alone if script has no arc. [Short-form storytelling](/en/blog/storytelling-en-video-corto) helps upstream.

## Pre-export checklist

- [ ] Hook visible frame 1
- [ ] Logo under 2 s total or outro only
- [ ] Manually reviewed captions
- [ ] Voice louder than music
- [ ] CTA last 5 s
- [ ] 9:16 1080×1920 minimum
- [ ] Safe areas OK
- [ ] Preview on real phone
- [ ] Licensed music
- [ ] Caption aligned with video CTA

## Internal vs external editing

**Internal works if:** 4–8 pieces/month, trained person, CapCut/Premiere templates

**Outsource if:** 12+ pieces, flat retention, motion graphics, multiple brand reviewers

## Brief that prevents mistakes

Clear brief reduces eighty percent of revisions. See [how to brief your video editor](/en/blog/como-briefear-a-tu-editor-de-video): exact duration, three rhythm references, mandatory on-screen copy, caption style, logo in/out, revision count.

## Recover published Reels with errors

1. **Slow hook:** Re-edit first 3 s, republish in 3–4 weeks
2. **No captions:** Burn subs, republish as "caption version"
3. **Missing CTA:** Pin comment CTA + story sticker instead of full re-edit

## Summary

**Business Reels editing mistakes** are predictable and fixable: slow intro, bad captions, unbalanced audio, excessive effects, wrong export and forgotten CTA. Before asking for more ad budget, audit post-production — recovered retention multiplies organic reach for free.
