## Short answer

This guide covers **derechos de uso contenido ugc** for brands operating in Colombia. UGC (user-generated content) works because it feels like a peer recommendation—not a TV commercial. Define your goal, write a clear brief, hire aligned creators, and measure retention and CTR before scaling spend.

Read the full [UGC creator guide Colombia](/es/blog/creador-ugc-colombia-guia) for context.

## Why it matters in Colombia

Colombian consumers spend significant time on Instagram and TikTok. They skip polished ads that feel disconnected from daily life. Native vertical video—with local accent, real environments and honest product demos—builds trust faster than catalog photography.

## Step-by-step process

1. **Set one KPI** — CTR, retention, saves or DMs
2. **Write a one-page brief** — product promise, audience, hook, must-say / must-not, 9:16 format, CTA, deadline, usage rights
3. **Filter creators** by niche and mobile aesthetics, not follower count alone
4. **Review** for brand, legal claims and audio quality (max 2 rounds)
5. **Distribute** organically, in paid ads, on PDP and WhatsApp where allowed

See [how to hire a UGC creator](/es/blog/como-contratar-creador-ugc) and [UGC brief template](/es/blog/brief-ugc-plantilla).

## Formats that perform

| Format | Length | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Honest unboxing | 20–40 s | Ecommerce, ads |
| Routine / POV | 15–30 s | Beauty, food, apps |
| Problem → solution | 25–45 s | Services, tech |
| Soft comparison | 30–60 s | Retail, fashion |

## Organic vs paid

Organic UGC lives on brand and creator profiles without media spend. Paid UGC amplifies winning creatives on Meta and TikTok. Most SMEs see better results combining both—see [organic vs paid UGC](/es/blog/ugc-organico-vs-pagado).

## Usage rights

Without an explicit clause, assume organic use on brand channels only. For ads and whitelisting, contract duration, territory, platforms and any extra fee. Details: [UGC usage rights](/es/blog/derechos-de-uso-contenido-ugc).

## Rates (orientation only)

UGC pricing in Colombia varies by deliverables and ad rights. Simple 15–30 s pieces are quoted per video; monthly packs (6–10 pieces) usually include a volume discount. See [UGC creator rates Colombia](/es/blog/tarifas-creador-ugc-colombia) and [pricing](/es/tarifas).

## Common mistakes

- Vague brief → generic interchangeable content
- Running ads without paid usage rights
- Over-editing until the piece loses native feel
- Scaling volume before documenting process
- Choosing creators only by follower count

## Metrics to track

- 3-second retention and midpoint retention
- Ad CTR vs previous creatives
- Save rate and qualitative comments
- Cost per asset vs cost per result

## Questions brands ask

SMEs often ask whether UGC fits their specific vertical. It works best when the product can be **shown in use** within 45 seconds: consumer goods, beauty, food, accessible fashion, apps and visual services.

Another common question: Bogotá vs Medellín vs remote creators. For national brands, mixing cities adds diversity. For hyperlocal businesses, prioritize creators in that city for street and accent recognition.

## Quality checklist before approval

- Clean audio — viewers who enable sound penalize bad quality
- Product visible in first 5 seconds
- Readable subtitles with enough contrast
- CTA aligned to goal — DM, bio link, code or local visit
- No prohibited claims in regulated categories
- Native 9:16 export without black bars

## Meta and TikTok paid placement

Native UGC often integrates into conversion campaigns with better CTR than static banners:

- Upload 3–5 variants per ad set
- Run 5–7 days before killing a creative
- Different hook per variant (price, problem, proof)
- Geo-target Colombia when product is not national
- Respect signed ad usage rights
