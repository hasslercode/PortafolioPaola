## Definition

**Community management** is moderating, replying and activating conversation on social — publishing and handling comments/DMs.

For brands and SMBs in Colombia, Community management connects to strategy, production and measurement.

## Why it matters

Understanding Community management avoids vendor misunderstandings and vanity metrics without sales impact.

## Practical example

An SMB tags each weekly piece with Community management and reviews in a 30-minute Friday meeting.
