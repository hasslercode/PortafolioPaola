## Definition

**UGC** is content created by real users or customers — not the brand team — showing product or service in authentic context.

For brands and SMBs in Colombia, UGC (User Generated Content) connects to strategy, production and measurement.

## Why it matters

Understanding UGC (User Generated Content) avoids vendor misunderstandings and vanity metrics without sales impact.

## Practical example

An SMB tags each weekly piece with UGC (User Generated Content) and reviews in a 30-minute Friday meeting.
