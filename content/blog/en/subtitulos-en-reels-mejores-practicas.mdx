## Short answer

**Reels captions best practices** start from one truth: much of your audience in Colombia watches without sound on transit, at work or in bed. If the message does not work by reading the screen, you lose retention even with a good script.

Integrate captions in your [how to edit retaining Reels](/en/blog/como-editar-reels-que-retienen) pipeline and scale with [content production](/en/services/content-production). Ranges on [pricing](/en/pricing).

## Why silence dominates consumption

Platform data confirms mute-first consumption in vertical feed. For consumer brands — Coca-Cola, Starbucks, H&M, TOTTO, Cine Colombia — short video must communicate promise and proof without depending on trending audio.

Retail campaigns with **+1.3M organic views** in 90 days (documented contexts like Parque Alegra) combine visual hook, clear caption and CTA. Fast comprehension reduces swipe-away.

## Burned vs native vs SRT

| Method | Advantage | Disadvantage |
|---|---|---|
| Burned (burn-in) | Full design control | Not editable post-export |
| Native IG | Automatic accessibility | Limited style |
| SRT sidecar | Flexible in some flows | Not always visible on Reels |

For founders and SMEs in Colombia, **burned with template** is usually most effective.

## Typography and contrast

**Font:** bold sans-serif. Avoid thin serif or decorative script.

**Size:** minimum legible on mobile — test on real device.

**Contrast:** light text on dark semi-transparent background, or dark text with white stroke. Never gray on gray.

**Length:** max two lines per block.

## Safe areas in 9:16

Instagram covers bottom zone with buttons. Leave ~15% bottom margin free of critical text. Detail in [9:16 format guide](/en/blog/formato-9-16-guia-emprendedores).

## Highlighted keywords

Highlight 1–3 meaningful words per block. If entire caption is yellow, nothing stands out. Align with [3-second hooks](/en/blog/hooks-primeros-3-segundos-tiktok).

## Sync and reading rhythm

Eye reads slower than ear. Adjust caption in/out to match speech. In numbered lists, show number when spoken.

## Auto caption correction workflow

1. Generate in CapCut, Descript or Premiere.
2. Fix spelling and accents (critical in Spanish).
3. Fix brand, city, product names.
4. Split long blocks.
5. Apply brand visual template.
6. Mute preview on mobile — does full story work?

## Captions for sales Reels

Commercial pieces should repeat hook promise and final CTA in text. See [sales Reels script](/en/blog/guion-para-reels-de-ventas).

## Common mistakes in Colombia

1. Text covering presenter face entire video.
2. Captions hiding product in demo.
3. Trendy illegible font.
4. Not captioning B-roll with key spoken info.
5. Confusing post caption with on-screen subtitles.
6. Forgetting dark/light mode preview.

## Captions by brand tone

**Premium/luxury:** fewer on-screen words, subtle animation.

**Disruptive/youth:** vivid colors, large keywords, pop sync to cuts.

**B2B/consulting:** high contrast, minimal decoration, highlighted numbers.

## Tool comparison

CapCut auto is fast but Spanish spelling imperfect. Descript offers text-based editing. Premiere gives pro control. Recommended flow: auto + human review + brand template.

## Trends and viral audio

When trending audio does not explain brand message, captions carry 100% of meaning. Product must appear in first 2 seconds if using unrelated trend.

## Series continuity

Weekly series ("Part 2", "Day 5"): episode number in first caption block builds habit. Keep position and style identical across pieces.
