## Definition

**Storytelling** is narrating with structure to connect emotionally and drive action — compressed to 15–60 seconds in short video.

For brands and SMBs in Colombia, Storytelling connects to strategy, production and measurement.

## Why it matters

Understanding Storytelling avoids vendor misunderstandings and vanity metrics without sales impact.

## Practical example

An SMB tags each weekly piece with Storytelling and reviews in a 30-minute Friday meeting.
