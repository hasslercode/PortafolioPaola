## Definition

**B-roll** is complementary footage illustrating the narration — product in use, environment, detail.

For brands and SMBs in Colombia, B-roll connects to strategy, production and measurement.

## Why it matters

Understanding B-roll avoids vendor misunderstandings and vanity metrics without sales impact.

## Practical example

An SMB tags each weekly piece with B-roll and reviews in a 30-minute Friday meeting.
