## Definition

**Content batching** groups similar tasks in blocks to reduce context switching and increase monthly output.

For brands and SMBs in Colombia, Content batching connects to strategy, production and measurement.

## Why it matters

Understanding Content batching avoids vendor misunderstandings and vanity metrics without sales impact.

## Practical example

An SMB tags each weekly piece with Content batching and reviews in a 30-minute Friday meeting.
