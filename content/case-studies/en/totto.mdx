## TOTTO in the feed: when functionality becomes desire

TOTTO is a recognized Colombian brand for backpacks, accessories and functional product with its own style identity. In physical store, customers touch texture, test compartments and compare colors; on TikTok or Reels that advantage disappears if content stops at product-on-white-background photos. The challenge was **showing style and functionality without static catalog**, inside Parque Alegra in Barranquilla, and driving organic discovery through real-use storytelling.

The featured piece reached **1.27 million organic views** — documented on the published post. It is among the highest volumes in the Parque Alegra portfolio, comparable to Coca-Cola's featured piece and above categories like fashion or coffee on their respective highlights. That does not make TOTTO a magic formula; it shows **functional product + visual hook + real location** can compete for attention with traditionally “more viral” categories.

## Context: tenant inside Parque Alegra

Content was produced within **Parque Alegra**, operated by Parque Arauco. TOTTO operates as a mall tenant; the piece belongs to campaigns that together exceeded **1.3M+ organic views in 90 days** across the documented ecosystem.

With the same hub transparency: **TOTTO is a mall tenant; there is not always a direct contract between content creator and brand**. The case describes in-location product and experience content with measurable organic results.

## Problem and objective

Backpacks and accessories compete against dance, recipes, news and pure entertainment. Product is not inherently “dramatic” in three seconds — unless narrative activates **problem and solution**: organization, style, daily routine, travel, school, work. The problem was avoiding SKU slideshow and building sequence where product stars in a **credible use moment**.

Objective: **organic discovery and product desire** combining functionality (compartments, durability, comfort) with style aspiration (color, outfit, usage context).

## Process and results

Workflow: **product and angle selection** — one routine or outing angle per video; **on-location capture** inside TOTTO at Parque Alegra with hands opening compartments and walking with product; **vertical editing** with retention pace and minimal on-screen text clarifying functional benefit; **publishing and tracking** toward **1.27M documented views**.

Qualitative results:

- **Functional and aspirational narrative** — product is understood and desired.
- **Social proof for accessories category** — not only fashion or beverages can sustain high volume in Parque Alegra ecosystem.
- **Transferable method** for D2C, gifts, premium stationery and regional luggage brands.

1.27M is not guaranteed for every backpack in every city. Execution criteria transfer: real use, clear hook, honest location, paced edit.

## Relationship to the Parque Alegra hub

The TOTTO piece did not exist in isolation: it shared calendar space with content from other mall brands, enabling **cross social proof** — audiences discovering one brand through the mall and others arriving via TOTTO while seeing format consistency across the hub. That network effect is part of Parque Alegra case value and why documenting known brands separately still matters.
